About Kart Stats

There are 23 karts in Mario Kart 8 Deluxe, each with their own unique stats that affect their performance on the track.
These stats include speed, acceleration, weight, handling, traction, and mini-turbo (hidden in game).
Vehicle types are also controlled differently, with bikes having more sharper drifts mechanic than karts.
